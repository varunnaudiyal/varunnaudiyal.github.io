Varun Naudiyal

5th Year BS-MS student in Physics, IISER-Bhopal

Hi! I am an aspiring astrophysicist, currently in my final year at IISER Bhopal. My current work focuses on using multi-messenger observations of electromagnetic transients and gravitational waves to constrain the still-unknown nuclear equation of states that governs the interior physics of neutron stars. I approach this problem from several directions, ranging from constructing sequences of quasi-equilibrium configurations to using physics-informed machine learning techniques.

Aside from this, I am also interested in numerical relativity simulations of binary neutron star (BNS) mergers and tidal disruption events, essentially relativistic hydrodynamics and all that comes with it: jets, disks etc etc.

I’d also love to explore accretion physics, BNS progenitors and the common-envelope phase, as well as the early stages of stellar formation, particularly protostars and protostellar disks. More broadly, I’m interested in computational approaches to astrophysics and in understanding how we can connect theoretical models and simulations with observations.

Outside research I like reading fiction, with a particular love for fantasy. The "Wheel of Time" and "A Song Of Ice and Fire" (aka Game of thrones) series are my favourites. I also like to draw, though I haven't done much of it recently. I also play the keyboard at times :).
