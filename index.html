Varun Naudiyal

5th Year BS-MS student in Physics, IISER-Bhopal

Hi! I am an aspiring astrophysicist, currently in my final year at IISER Bhopal. My research interests lie in the field of Multi-Messenger Astrophysics where we use the combination of both Electro- magnetic and Gravitational Wave observations to explore various transient phenomena in the universe. My interests lie in exploring the physics of compact objects like Neutron Stars and their evolution in various environments. Alongside this, I am also interested in understanding the evolution of Stellar Binary systems and how their interactions can lead to the formation of compact binaries, whose mergers we can ultimately observe through gravitational waves and electromagnetic counterparts such as kilonovae.

My Master's Thesis focuses on using Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to constrain the equation of state (EOS) of neutron-star matter beyond nuclear saturation density. Standard PINNs can struggle to model discontinuities, so a central theme of my work is extending existing coupled PINN frameworks to incorporate first-order phase transitions in dense matter, alongside Bayesian inference. Through this, I aim to investigate what phase-transition signatures are supported by neutron-star observables, while quantifying uncertainties in the inferred EOS rather than obtaining only a single deterministic reconstruction.
