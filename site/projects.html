Nuclear Equation of State: RMF/QHD Implementation in Neutron Stars

Studying Quantum Hadrodynamics and Relativistic Mean Field theory as a framework for dense nuclear matter beyond saturation density. Implementing a beta-equilibrated nuclear matter EOS incorporating the baryon octet coupled to σ, ω, and ρ mesons, with leptons enforcing charge neutrality, and extending the framework toward hyperon-rich matter and a hadron-quark hybrid EOS.

Jun 2026 – Jul 2026 · Summer Project, Dr. Ritam Mallick, IISER-B

Rotational Energy Budget of Supramassive Neutron Star Merger Remnants

Studied the physics of rotating neutron stars and turning point theorem, which identifies onset of secular instabilities. Used the RNS code to construct sequences of rotating supramassive neutron star configurations, spanning the mass-shedding limit to the turning point of secular instability, and computed the extractable rotational kinetic energy released during spin-down collapse. Compared this energy budget against the observed kilonova energy of GW170817 and checked the consistency of realistic EOS models against this energy budget.

Exploring Neutron Stars in R2 Gravity

Derived the modified gravitational field equations for the Starobinsky R2 model and extended the Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equations to f(R) gravity. Numerically integrated the modified stellar structure equations via a shooting method to map mass-radius configurations, and diagnosed the scalaron fine-tuning problem inherent to f(R) stellar structure.
